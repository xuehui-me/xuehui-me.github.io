About Me

I am a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. I study how plants make bioactive natural products, and engineer enzymes and microbes to produce them. My work combines pathway discovery, protein engineering, and machine learning.

I completed my PhD research at CEMPS, Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2026, advised by Prof. Yong Wang, with joint doctoral training at ECUST, co-advised by Prof. Yu-Hong Ren.
